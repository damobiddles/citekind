Introducing Citekind: helping B2B tech get named in AI answers

Your buyers have started asking AI assistants who to shortlist. Citekind exists to make sure the answer includes you.

DBDamien Bidmead, Founder · 17 November 2026 · 5 min read

For most of my career, being found meant ranking on Google. You chose your keywords, built your pages, earned your links and watched the traffic arrive. That model hasn't disappeared, but it's no longer where research starts for a lot of B2B buyers.

A CISO weighing up managed detection providers, or a CFO comparing payments platforms, is now just as likely to open ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Copilot and ask a straight question: who should I be talking to? The assistant replies with a short, confident list. If you're on it, you're in the conversation before sales has picked up the phone. If you're not, you've lost a deal you'll never see in your analytics.

That's the problem Citekind is built to solve.

What Citekind does

Citekind is an AI search and content studio for B2B cybersecurity and fintech scale-ups. We help companies get found, cited and recommended by the AI assistants their buyers use, including Google's AI Overviews.

The discipline is often called answer engine optimisation, or AEO. It builds on good SEO, but the goal shifts. Instead of ranking a page, you're trying to become a source an AI model trusts enough to name, and to make sure that when it does, it describes you accurately.

In practice that comes down to three things:

  • Clarity. AI models need to understand exactly what you do, who it's for and how you differ. Vague positioning gets you left out or lumped into the wrong category.
  • Specific, well-structured content. Assistants draw on content that answers real buyer questions directly, with detail they can quote.
  • Corroboration. What you say about yourself carries more weight when reviews, media coverage, analysts and partners say it too.

Three ways we work

1. The AI Visibility Audit

Every engagement starts here. It's a fixed-price, two-week diagnostic. We agree your category, competitors and the buyer questions that matter, then test those prompts across the major assistants and audit the content they draw on.

You get a benchmark of your visibility, your share of voice against up to five named competitors, a view of how AI describes you (including what it gets wrong), a review of your site's content and technical readiness, a map of the third-party sources AI trusts in your space, and a prioritised 90-day plan. You can run that plan yourself, hand it to your existing agency, or work with us on it.

2. The answer-ready content programme

For teams that want help acting on the audit, this is a monthly programme of expert-led content built to be cited: category pages, comparison and buyer-question content, thought leadership, and the third-party coverage that backs it up. It's built from interviews with your own experts, because the specific, first-hand detail they hold is exactly what generic content lacks. We track visibility quarterly so you can see what's moving.

3. Fractional CMO

Some scale-ups need more than content. They need positioning, narrative and a pipeline-focused marketing plan, without hiring a full-time CMO. I work as a senior part of your leadership team, with board-ready reporting and leadership of your team and agencies.

Why me, and why these sectors

I've spent more than 15 years in B2B technology marketing. Most recently I was CMO of Arco Cyber, a cybersecurity SaaS and services scale-up, through its acquisition by Sophos. Before that I led marketing in insurtech and anti-piracy SaaS, and started out agency-side in digital, video and SEO.

Cybersecurity and fintech share something important: senior, sceptical buyers making high-trust decisions, often in regulated settings. Those buyers do a lot of research before they speak to anyone, and they're quick to adopt tools that save them time. That makes them early AI-assistant users, and it makes accuracy matter. Being described wrongly to a CISO or a risk committee is arguably worse than not being mentioned at all.

The companies that win in AI answers aren't the loudest. They're the clearest, most specific and best corroborated.

That's work I know how to do, and it's why I started Citekind.
