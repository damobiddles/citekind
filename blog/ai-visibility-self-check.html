A 30-minute AI visibility check you can run this week

You don't need a tool or a budget to find out whether AI assistants recommend you. You need ten good questions, four browser tabs and half an hour.

DBDamien Bidmead, Founder · 17 November 2026 · 6 min read

Most marketing teams I speak to have a hunch about how they show up in AI answers, but haven't actually checked. Here's a quick, structured way to do it. It won't replace a full audit, but it will tell you whether you have a problem, and roughly how big.

Step 1: Write ten buyer questions (10 minutes)

The quality of this check depends on asking what your buyers actually ask, not what you'd like them to ask. Talk to sales if you can. Aim for a mix of these types:

  • Category: "What are the best [category] providers for [audience]?"
  • Use case: "How can a [company type] [solve problem]?"
  • Alternatives: "What are the alternatives to [your main competitor]?"
  • Regulation: "Which tools help with [DORA / NIS2 / Consumer Duty / PCI DSS]?"
  • Brand: "What does [your company] do, and who is it for?"

A few examples to get you started:

Prompt Which managed SOC providers are a good fit for a 500-person UK insurer?
Prompt What are the alternatives to [incumbent] for vulnerability management in a mid-market company?
Prompt Which platforms help UK fintechs prepare for DORA?
Prompt What does [your company] do, and how is it different from [competitor]?

Write them as a real person would type them, in full sentences, with the context a buyer would give (size, sector, location). Assistants tailor their answers to that context.

Step 2: Run them across four assistants (15 minutes)

Use ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot. Use a private or logged-out window where you can, so your own history doesn't skew the answers. For each answer, note four things:

QuestionWere you named?Was it accurate?Who else was named?Which sources were cited?
Best MDR for UK insurersNon/aCompetitor A, B, CReview site, trade article
What does [you] do?YesPartly: old categoryn/aYour 2023 press release
Alternatives to [incumbent]Yes, 4th of 5YesCompetitor A, DComparison blog, forum thread

Forty answers sounds like a lot, but you're skimming, not studying. A simple spreadsheet is plenty.

Step 3: Read the pattern (5 minutes)

Now step back and look for these signs.

You're missing from category questions but appear for brand questions

The assistant knows you exist but doesn't connect you with your category or audience. That's usually a clarity problem: your positioning isn't stated plainly or consistently enough across the web.

You're named but described wrongly

Look at where the wrong description comes from. It's often an old press release, an outdated directory listing or a review profile nobody has touched since a repositioning. These are some of the quickest fixes available.

The same competitors keep appearing

Open the pages the assistant cites for them. What are they publishing that you aren't? Often it's a straightforward comparison page, a regulation-specific guide, or a presence on one review site that dominates your category.

The same third-party sources keep getting cited

Make a list. These are the publications, review platforms and communities that shape answers in your market. If you're absent from most of them, that's where your PR and partnerships effort should go next.

A note on consistency

AI answers vary from one run to the next, so don't over-read a single result. If you can, run each question twice and look at the overall pattern. Then repeat the whole check every quarter to see whether things are moving.

What to do next

Even this quick check usually turns up two or three things you can fix in a week: an outdated boilerplate, a missing comparison page, a neglected review profile. Do those first.

Beyond that, the bigger questions are harder to answer from a spreadsheet: how you compare to competitors across a fuller set of buyer questions, which content and sources are really driving the answers, and what to prioritise over the next quarter. That's what the AI Visibility Audit is for.
