      About this check

      Does it use my ChatGPT or Perplexity account?

      No. The readiness check only reads your public website. The self-test opens ChatGPT and Perplexity in your own browser so you see the real answers. We never see them.

      Why does robots.txt matter for AI?

      AI assistants use crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and Claude-SearchBot to read pages when someone asks a question. If robots.txt blocks them, they can't read or cite you. Blocking training-only crawlers such as GPTBot is a separate choice, and we flag it differently.

      How accurate is the score?

      It's indicative. It checks the technical basics that decide whether AI can find and understand you. It can't see firewall rules, other pages, or what third-party sites say about you. Those are covered in the full audit.

      What do you do with my website address?

      We fetch your public pages once to run the checks. We don't store the results unless you ask for the full report.
