AI Visibility Audit: pricing, workflow and margins

How the two-week audit is priced, what the fee covers, how each audit gets run and with which tools, and what it earns.

Pricing

The public site doesn't name a price. It says the audit is a fixed fee agreed after a scoping call. The working range for a two-week audit for UK B2B tech firms, with a competitor benchmark and a 90-day roadmap, is £3,500 to £5,000. That's in line with what UK specialist consultancies charge for this kind of diagnostic.

What the fee covers

In the order it happens over the two weeks, matching what the site promises.

  1. Kick-off callDay 1Agree the category, up to five competitors, and the 30 to 50 buyer questions that matter.
  2. Prompt testingDays 2 to 5Run those questions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot and Google AI Overviews. Log every answer and cited source.
  3. Benchmark and share of voiceScore how often the client is named, how prominently, and against which competitors.
  4. Accuracy and positioning checkHow AI describes the client, including wrong facts, the wrong category, and missing differentiators.
  5. Site readiness reviewWhether the site's content, structure and technical setup are easy for AI to read and cite.
  6. Third-party source mapThe reviews, media, analyst and directory sources AI trusts, and where the client is absent from them.
  7. 90-day action planA prioritised list of quick wins and bigger moves, each with an owner and the effort involved.
  8. Readout sessionWeek 2A working session with the client's team, plus the written report.

Not included

Doing the fixes. That's the monthly programme.

How to run each audit

About 7 to 8 days of work spread across the two weeks.

  1. ScopingHalf a dayKick-off call plus a short intake form. The output is a sheet with the client's category, competitors and 30 to 50 buyer prompts.
    Tools: Zoom or Google Meet, Tally (free) for the intake form.
  2. Prompt testing2 to 3 daysRun each prompt in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot and Google search for AI Overviews. Use logged-out or clean sessions so results aren't skewed. Log each answer, the brands named and the sources cited.
    Tools: Google Sheets. Paid AI plans cost about £20 a month each. Once there are a few clients, an AI visibility tracker such as Otterly.ai or Peec AI (roughly £25 to £90 a month to start) automates this.
  3. Scoring1 dayCalculate mention rate, position and share of voice for the client and each competitor.
    Tools: the same Google Sheet.
  4. Accuracy reviewHalf a dayCompare what AI says about the client with the facts and their positioning. Flag errors and gaps.
  5. Site readiness1 dayCrawl the site, check structured data, and manually check robots.txt for AI crawlers.
    Tools: Screaming Frog (free up to 500 pages), Google Search Console (client access), Google's Rich Results Test.
  6. Source mapping1 dayTake the cited sources from prompt testing, then check G2, Capterra, analyst coverage, media and directories.
    Tools: Ahrefs or Semrush help but are optional at first.
  7. Plan and report1.5 daysTurn the findings into a prioritised 90-day plan.
    Tools: Google Slides or Docs, charts in Looker Studio (free).
  8. ReadoutHalf a dayA working session with the client's team, and hand over the report.

Margins

Very high on cash, because the main cost is time. Direct costs are about £100 to £150 per audit (tool subscriptions spread across clients).

PriceGross marginDay rate (~7.5 days)
Standard, £4,500~97%~£580 to £600
Launch, £2,950~95%~£380

The day rate is the more useful number, since time is the limit. At about two audits a month at £4,500, that's about £9,000 a month before tax.

Also budget for

  • Overheads such as insurance, accounting and software, perhaps £100 to £200 a month in total.
  • Corporation tax or income tax on what's left.
  • VAT once revenue passes £90k.
  • Lower margins on the monthly content programme if freelance writers are brought in.
