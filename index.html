FAQ

Questions people ask

What is AEO, and how is it different from SEO?

Answer engine optimisation (AEO) is the work of making your company the source AI assistants trust and cite. It builds on good SEO, but the emphasis shifts from ranking pages to being named in answers: clear positioning, specific and structured content, and consistent signals across the sites AI models rely on.

Can you guarantee we'll appear in ChatGPT?

No one honestly can, because the models change constantly. What we can do is measure where you stand, fix what's within your control, and track progress against competitors over time so you can see whether it's working.

Do we need to be in cybersecurity or fintech?

That's where the experience runs deepest, but the approach works for most B2B technology companies with a considered, multi-stakeholder sale. Get in touch and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.

Will this replace our current agency?

Usually not. The audit and roadmap are designed to work alongside your existing team and partners. Many clients use the plan to brief their SEO or PR agency more precisely.

How much does it cost?

The audit is a fixed fee, agreed upfront once we've confirmed scope on a short call. Ongoing programmes and fractional CMO work are priced monthly.
